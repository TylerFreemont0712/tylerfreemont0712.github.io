{%- comment -%} Remembers a language choice without hijacking navigation. The brief asked for a persisted language toggle. Auto-redirecting on a stored preference is the wrong way to honour that: it overrides someone who deliberately clicked through to the other language, breaks the back button, and serves crawlers different content than users. So the choice is recorded when the toggle is clicked, and a returning visitor whose stored preference differs from the page they landed on gets a dismissible bar with a link.
